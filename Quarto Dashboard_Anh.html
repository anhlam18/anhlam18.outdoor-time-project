The Big Model had lower prediction error and a higher \(R^2\) than the Smaller Model. However, both models had significant prediction errors, which suggests that education and the demographic and sunscreen variables explain only a limited amount of variation in outdoor time.

This dashboard uses data from the NHANES 2017–March 2020 Pre-Pandemic cycle. The analysis focuses on adults ages 20–59 who completed the examination.

The dashboard examines outdoor time on work or school days, education level, gender, and regular sunscreen use.

Outdoor Time: Minutes spent outdoors on a typical work or school day.

Education: Less than high school, High school/GED, Some college/AA, or College graduate or above.

Gender: Male or Female.

Regular Sunscreen Use: Participants who reported using sunscreen always or most of the time were classified as regular sunscreen users.
